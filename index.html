About EWARN

EWARN is a real-time emergency alert network that receives official National Weather Service products directly from NOAAPort, EMWIN, and CAP feeds, and delivers them to your phone, tablet, or desktop within seconds of issuance via Pushover — no ads, no accounts to babysit, no social-media noise.

Why Pushover?

Email and SMS were never designed for urgent warnings — messages get delayed, buried in an inbox, and give you no control over how they reach you. EWARN delivers alerts through Pushover, which pushes notifications to your devices the moment they're issued and supports true message priorities. We apply those priorities to every alert: routine advisories arrive quietly, while life-threatening warnings break through Do Not Disturb and repeat until you acknowledge them.

Note: We're not affiliated with Pushover — it's simply the best notification platform we've found for the job.
